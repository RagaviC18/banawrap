SEMI-AUTOMATIC BAGGING SYSTEM FOR ENHANCED PRE-HARVEST QUALITY IN BANANA CULTIVATION

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
India leads the world in banana production, but growing the fruit is only half the
battle; protecting the bunches once they form is just as critical to getting good quality and a
fair market price. Banana farmers deal with a lot of threats in the field such as insects,
disease, sunburn, physical damage from wind or handling, and birds pecking at the fruit.
Any of these can knock down both the yield and the quality of the harvest, and for farmers,
that translates directly into lost income.

In traditional and manual approaches, protective covers are provided to banana bunches so
that they can be protected from the above-mentioned field conditions. But banana bunches
are generally located at higher levels on the plant. Therefore, it is very difficult to put
protective covering manually on them. Ladders or manually operated tools have to be used
by farmers in order to reach banana bunches and put the protective cover on them. It is time-
consuming and a laborious process.

PROPOSED SOLUTION:
To address this problem, we propose a lightweight, easy-to-handle semi-automatic
banana bunch covering machine that uses a biodegradable banana bunch bag. This device
protects bunches from sunburn, insect attacks, and disease, improving the fruit&#39;s quality and
market value. Most importantly, it lets farmers cover bunches at different heights without
needing a ladder.

The tool is built using a telescopic cable mast extension mechanism, an upper bagging head,
a double plate bag releasing mechanism, a double-plate bag holding arm, a linear actuator, a
protective banana bunch bag, and a gear motor.

OBJECTIVES:
Reduce manual effort in banana bunch bagging.
Minimize the need for climbing ladders.
Improve the ease of bag placement.
Develop an affordable and practical agricultural solution.
Support safer and more efficient farming practices.

PROJECT STATUS
Semi-automatic prototype developed.
Mechanical design and fabrication completed.
Further development and improvements planned.

PROJECT VISION
To make banana cultivation safer, smarter and more farmer-friendly through practical engineering innovation.
